How to Play

Overview

Omaha Hi-Lo (also called PLO8 or Omaha 8-or-Better) is a split-pot poker game where the pot is divided between the best high hand and the best qualifying low hand.

Dealing

Each player receives 4 hole cards. Five community cards are dealt across the flop, turn, and river.

Key Rule

You must use exactly 2 of your hole cards and exactly 3 community cards to make your hand. This applies separately for high and low.

Pot Split

  • High half: Best standard poker hand wins
  • Low half: Best qualifying low hand wins
  • If no player qualifies for low, high hand takes the entire pot
  • One player can "scoop" by winning both halves

Qualifying Low

To qualify for the low pot, you need 5 unpaired cards ranked 8 or below. Aces count as low. Straights and flushes don't disqualify a low hand.

Best low: A-2-3-4-5 (the "wheel")

Worst qualifying low: 4-5-6-7-8

Betting Rounds

  • Preflop: After hole cards are dealt
  • Flop: After 3 community cards
  • Turn: After 4th community card
  • River: After 5th community card

Hand Rankings (High)

  2. Straight Flush
  3. Four of a Kind
  4. Full House
  5. Flush
  6. Straight
  7. Three of a Kind

  9. One Pair
